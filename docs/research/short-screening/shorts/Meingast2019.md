# Meingast2019 — Extended stellar systems in the solar neighborhood. I. The tidal tails of the Hyades
## Object
Hyades open cluster and its tidal tails; tidal tails of disk clusters.
## Kind of work
Observation (Gaia DR2 astrometry), compared by eye with published models; no own simulations.
## Data and methods
- Gaia DR2 6D sample within 200 pc, Bailer-Jones distances: search sample.
- Galactocentric cylindrical velocities (Astropy) + 2.5 km/s velocity cut + spatial-density cut: member selection (238 stars).
- 3D covariance ellipsoids: extent, shape, warp.
- PARSEC isochrones + 2D KS test: coevality check; isochrone interpolation: masses.
- Published N-body models (Chumak et al. 2005 contour, Ernst et al. 2011): reference tail shape, length, velocity-dispersion profile; Röser et al. 2011 census: completeness.
## Quantities
Tail extent/thickness (~200 x 25 pc), axis ratios, position-angle warp, cumulative 3D velocity-dispersion profile vs radius, tail length per arm, stellar mass spectrum, mean BP-RP colour vs distance (mass segregation).
## End Arguments
- e_tails: Gaia DR2 reveals S-shaped Hyades tidal tails (~200 pc) matching predicted shape and dispersion; length truncated by selection.
- e_massseg: no mass segregation between cluster and tails within limits, likely due to Gaia RV sensitivity.
