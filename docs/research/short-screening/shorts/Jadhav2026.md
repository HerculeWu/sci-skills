# Jadhav2026 — The white dwarf population of open clusters and their tidal tails: Tracers of contamination and stellar interactions
## Object
White dwarfs (WDs) in open-cluster cores and tidal tails; 80 clusters, notably Hyades, Pleiades, Praesepe, NGC 2682 (M67), Ruprecht 147.
## Kind of work
Observation (catalogue analysis) plus N-body simulation.
## Data and methods
- Nbody6 library (Dvořáková+2024; M62–M8000, binaries, gas expulsion, Galactic potential): WD birth sites, escape, segregation, core WD fraction
- Energy/angular-momentum reference point for dissolved clusters
- 4 Gaia OC–WD catalogues; GALEX, SDSS, PanSTARRS, 2MASS, WISE, UVIT photometry
- Camisassa DA cooling models (CMD, Monte Carlo errors), Koester SEDs in VOSA; cluster cooling sequence from M26 IFMR + Parsec
- Galpy, ClusterTools listed as tools
## Quantities
WD mass, cooling age, Teff, log g, radius; contamination rates; WD/MS ratio, core WD fraction; distance from cluster vs cooling time; progenitor masses.
## End Arguments
- e-catalogues: Gaia cluster catalogues ≳13% contaminated at WD magnitudes, tail catalogues far more (>48%)
- e-pleiades: reported Pleiades tails mostly contaminants, not genuine
- e-tail-origin: ~83% of tail WDs formed in the cluster and escaped
- e-deficit: observed core WD fractions below simulations; WD deficit persists
- e-binary: 63 candidate binary-origin WDs
- e-ifmr: 47 new IFMR candidates, incl. 2–2.7 M⊙ progenitors
