# Oh2020 — Kinematic modelling of clusters with Gaia: the death throes of the Hyades
## Object
Hyades open cluster (inside r_t≈10 pc) and its tidal tails.
## Kind of work
Observation with Bayesian kinematic modelling; simple analytic models; no own simulations.
## Data and methods
- Gaia DR2 astrometry + partial RVs; members from Gaia Collab. 2018, tails from Meingast & Alves 2019, Röser et al. 2019.
- Hierarchical forward model (code kinesis, Stan/PyStan NUTS): mean velocity, dispersion matrix, 9-component linear velocity gradient, background mixture; exact perspective treatment.
- Mock catalogues: validate method and perspective handling.
- Jeans model of a prolate Plummer spheroid: equilibrium dispersion.
- Power-law mass-loss model: initial mass, lifetime.
- MIST isochrone: colour-to-mass for tail/cluster masses.
- Ernst et al. 2011 N-body results (cited): mass, mass loss, tail length.
- Epicyclic theory (Just et al. 2009): tail length, clumps.
## Quantities
Velocity dispersions/anisotropy, rotation and shear, Oort-like A,B,C,K of tails, vertex deviation, membership fraction, tail/cluster mass ratio, mass-loss rate, remaining lifetime, tail length.
## End Arguments
- e-cluster-kinematics: cluster near-isotropic, no rotation, ≈2σ shear
- e-death-throes: Hyades super-virial, tidally heated, dissolves in ≲30 Myr
- e-tails-kinematics: tails kinematically distinct; A,B ≠ local Oort values
- e-tail-extent: tails ≈400 pc long, epicyclic clumps at y≈±154 pc
