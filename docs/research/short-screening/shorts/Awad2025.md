# Awad2025 — S5: deep spectroscopy of the tidal tails of NGC 1261 and NGC 1904
## Object
Globular clusters NGC 1261 and NGC 1904; their tidal tails and off-orbit, cross-shaped extra-tidal features.
## Kind of work
Observation (spectroscopy + astrometry) compared with N-body runs.
## Data and methods
- S5 AAT/AAOmega spectra (rvspecfit) + Gaia DR3 proper motions; DECam/DES DR2 g,r CMDs with PARSEC isochrones: membership check, distance offsets.
- Gaussian mixture model (stream + background) fit with emcee: membership probabilities; refits without one proper motion and posterior draws as robustness tests; KDE density maps.
- Orbit integration in MWPotential2014 + moving LMC (dynamical friction, reflex): orbits, phases, Jacobi radii along orbit (King formula).
- Gadget-3 N-body runs (King models, 1e5 particles, last ~1 Gyr only): predicted debris sky positions, v_gsr, proper motions, distances.
## Quantities
Membership probabilities; v_gsr, proper-motion, [Fe/H] tracks; velocity and metallicity dispersions; pericentres; Jacobi radii today and at pericentre; Δv_gsr pattern; distance gradient across inner tails.
## End Arguments
- e-members: extra-tidal members over ~10 r_J confirm Shipp+2018 features.
- e-ngc1904: the cross is inner (toward Galactic centre) + outer tails of a cluster near apocentre.
- e-ngc1261: inner tails follow orbit; double stream not reproduced (bar or overlapping streams?).
- e-outlook: method extends to other multi-tail GCs (NGC 288, NGC 2298).
