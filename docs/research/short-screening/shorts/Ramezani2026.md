# Ramezani2026 — Tidal Tails of Galactic Open Clusters: A Review of Methods, Evidence, and Sources of Disagreement
## Object
Tidal tails, coronae and extra-tidal populations of Galactic open clusters: Hyades, Praesepe, Coma Ber, Blanco 1, Pleiades, NGC 2516, M 67, NGC 752, IC 4756, COIN-Gaia 13; surveys of 21-476 OCs.
## Kind of work
Review; no new data, runs or computation.
## Data and methods
Compares published Gaia DR2/EDR3/DR3 tail studies. Reviews membership methods: convergent point/SCCP, DBSCAN, HDBSCAN, GMM, kNN/ML-MOC, PCA+GMM, StarGO, Forest Fire Clustering; orbit integration in a Galactic potential and N-body matching (Kupper 2010, Weis 2025, Kos 2024) as validation. Jacobi radius from Oort constants. Uses Jadhav 2025 catalogue grading (simulated clusters).
## Quantities
Tail extent (one-sided, tip-to-tip, projected vs 3D), core/tail member counts, Jacobi radius, detection rates, contamination, completeness.
## End Arguments
- e_status: Hyades is the benchmark tail; Praesepe, Coma Ber strong; extended populations common.
- e_nonbinary: tail catalogues are not binary detections; extent and counts depend on method, release, dimensionality.
- e_hierarchy: evidence levels 0-4 (spatial, astrometric, photometric, dynamical, spectroscopic).
- e_reporting: six quantities every tail catalogue should report.
- e_future: multi-method benchmark on one Gaia sample, calibrated on injected simulated clusters, plus DR4, spectroscopy, N-body.
