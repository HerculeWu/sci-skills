# Kroupa2022 — Asymmetrical tidal tails of open star clusters: stars crossing their cluster's práh challenge Newtonian gravitation
## Object
Tidal tails of open clusters in the Galactic disk: Hyades, Praesepe, Coma Berenices, NGC 752 (also COIN-Gaia 13); Newtonian vs Milgromian (MOND) gravity.
## Kind of work
Mix: compiled observations, simulations, analytic theory.
## Data and methods
- Published Gaia DR2 convergent-point and eDR3 CCP tail members: leading/trailing star counts.
- Backward orbit integration (time-symmetric Hermite, Allen & Santillan potential) and an analytic snapshot-eccentricity formula: cluster orbits.
- PoR (Phantom of Ramses, QUMOND adaptive mesh, staticparts patch): collision-less Plummer clusters, 2500-20000 Msun, 1 Gyr in a static-particle disk galaxy, Milgromian vs Newtonian (a0 -> 0, plus dark halo).
- Analytic external-field, relaxation and precession estimates; cited Newtonian direct n-body results.
## Quantities
Tail ratio q = N_lead/N_trail (50-200 pc) and q(t); 3D velocity dispersion; G_eff; cluster lifetimes; spin; orbital eccentricity growth; Küpper overdensity distances.
## End Arguments
- e-main: tail asymmetry challenges Newton, arises in MOND
- e-kuepper: MOND predicts asymmetric Küpper spacing
- e-spin-prediction: old clusters spin against their orbit
- e-suicide: MOND clusters self-destroy via feedback
- e-lifetime: MOND clusters dissolve faster
- e-future-work: need better tail data, collisional MOND code
